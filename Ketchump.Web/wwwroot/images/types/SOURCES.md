Resolution notes: Kanto artwork is 1280x1280 from Pokemon Project Studio. All eight Johto badges use 1200x1200 official Pokemon Center product photos from the Johto Premium Gym Badge Set (https://www.pokemoncenter.com/product/710-03202/johto-premium-gym-badge-set). Dark (522x522) and Fairy (256x256) retain their existing full-size artwork. Assets are not artificially upscaled.
